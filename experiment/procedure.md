## Procedure

Follow the instructions given below to perform the experiments:-

* Step 1:- Click on the button START. A page appears with a dialogue box asking for your name.

   <div align="center">
    <img src="images/instruction1.png" alt="Large Scale Fading">  
      </div>

* Step 2:- Enter your name then Click Ok.

    <div align="center">
    <img src="images/instruction2.png" alt="Large Scale Fading">  
      </div>

* Step3:- Select the parameters (e.g: Reuse ,Environment, Beamwidth,Carrier frequency etc.)

    <div align="center">
    <img src="images/instruction3.png" alt="Large Scale Fading">  
      </div>

* Step4:- Click on "CDF plot" button to get plot of CDF vs SINR(dB)

   <div align="center">
    <img src="images/instruction4.png" alt="Large Scale Fading">  
      </div>

* Step5:- Click on the "Report" button to generate PDF report of the experiment.

    <div align="center">
    <img src="images/instruction5.png" alt="Large Scale Fading">  
      </div>

* Step6:-After generation of the Report you will get following message.

    <div align="center">
    <img src="images/instruction6.png" alt="Large Scale Fading">  
      </div>

* Step7:- Click on the "Ok" and you will get your Report.

    <div align="center">
    <img src="images/instruction7.png" alt="Large Scale Fading">  
      </div>

* Step8:- To Redo the experiment click on "RESET" button.
